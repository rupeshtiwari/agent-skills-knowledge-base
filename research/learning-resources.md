## Suggested Learning Sequence

1. Read the standard and your host's official skills documentation.
2. Study a small vendor example without installing it blindly.
3. Read the benchmark comparisons in this repository.
4. Watch a primary-speaker talk and compare its claims with current documentation.
5. Complete the design worksheet for one task.
6. Create positive, negative and failure cases before writing a large instruction file.
7. Measure your first version and keep a failure journal.

No paid subscription or purchase is required to use this repository. External resources may require accounts or payment; availability can change.
