# Research Method and Coverage

Research date: 3 November 2026. This is a broad initial evidence map, not a systematic review or exhaustive internet archive.

## Search Scope

Searched official OpenAI, Claude/Anthropic, Kiro and Cursor documentation; the Agent Skills standard; skill benchmarks and reliability papers; university thesis records; author/publisher book resources; primary-speaker video/course listings; and public issue reports in vendor repositories.

Representative queries included: `Codex skills SKILL.md`, `agent skills best practices Claude Kiro Cursor official documentation`, `agent skills benchmark SkillsBench`, `LLM agents thesis skills reliability university dissertation`, and `Claude Code issues skills trigger`.

## Selection Rules

Prefer official documentation for product behavior, original papers for results, institutional records for theses and firsthand issue reports for reported defects. Exclude copied book PDFs, unverifiable claims and promotional lists without enough provenance. Preserve useful unreviewed leads with explicit labels rather than describing them as reviewed evidence.

## Evidence Labels

- Documented behavior: official product/specification source, within the stated surface.
- Research finding: measured result within the source's experiment; version retained.
- Community report: a person's observation or hypothesis, not independently reproduced here.
- Engineering recommendation: this repository's synthesis or proposed test design.
- Unreviewed lead: discovered resource that needs deeper inspection.

## Important Gaps

The paper summaries are based on abstracts or publisher summaries, not full-method replication. The videos were not watched. One book and two theses are indexed, not read in full. Community bugs were not reproduced. No live skills were executed across the four platforms. Search coverage is English-led and not exhaustive. External link health was checked through research retrieval only where noted; no complete automated link crawl was performed.

## Next Research Priorities

Review benchmark full texts and verifier designs; inspect current host changelogs; run a small cross-host skill experiment; add more primary-speaker recordings with transcript-supported notes; expand book/thesis coverage when directly relevant; and reproduce a minimal discovery failure in a controlled environment.

No promise of a complete market survey or automatic freshness is made. The catalogue and contribution workflow are designed to grow the collection with evidence.
